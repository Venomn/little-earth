# Data attribution

Land outlines: Natural Earth, 1:110m physical land dataset, public domain.

- Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
- Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- Retrieved: 2026-10-15. Local copy: `Assets/land.geojson`.

Wool material (`Assets/wool-material.png`), stylized global UV artwork (`Sources/LittleEarth/Resources/wool-world.png`), and transparent clouds (`wool-cloud.png`, `wool-cloud-rounded.png` in the same resource directory) are OpenAI image-generation outputs based on the selected art direction. Their prompts are saved in `output/art-direction/`. The global UV artwork was generated from a project-created Natural Earth map and the chosen concept reference. It is artistic geography, not authoritative cartography. Fiber geometry and lights are generated by the project; build-time texture processing derives matching relief and normal resources from the UV artwork.
Concept images in `output/art-direction` were generated using OpenAI image generation and are design references only; the app does not display those static images as its globe.

## Weather data

Weather data by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- API documentation: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs
- Terms: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
- This personal prototype uses current model weather at 186 regional sample coordinates. It converts interval precipitation amounts to hourly rates and interpolates cloud cover for an artistic globe display. It is not a satellite image or a city weather observation.

The v0.3 art revision adds `wool-world-v3.png` and `wool-tuft-v3.png`, generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool using the approved weather-felt reference. Source prompts: `output/art-direction/felt-v3-prompts.json`. The cloud tufts are arranged as layered image sprites in 3D; the globe is a deformable mesh with texture-derived land relief and fine normal detail.

## Regional culture

Region discovery uses Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0 polygons, public domain. Retrieved 2026-10-16 from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson and stored in `Sources/EarthCore/Resources/countries.geojson`. Generalized geography is used only to associate a click with representative cultural reading; it is not a statement about disputed boundaries.

`Sources/EarthCore/Resources/culture.json` contains original short Chinese summaries and their English translations curated on 2026-10-16 from UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, Japan National Tourism Organization, Tourism Australia, France.fr, Smithsonian National Museum of American History, and United Nations Brazil. Every entry records its source name and URL. Full articles, source images, and logos are not redistributed. Month ranges and festival reading windows are editorial selection rules, not verified live event schedules.

## City light anchors

City anchors are derived from Natural Earth 1:110m populated places (public domain), retrieved 2026-10-16: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_populated_places_simple.geojson . The local `Sources/EarthCore/Resources/cities.json` retains name, point coordinates and population estimates for visual sizing. Dataset description: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-populated-places/ . These are static city anchors, not current population or power usage. Nearby small lights and their glow are procedurally generated artwork, filtered by the felt land mask.

## Marine reading

Marine region polygons: Natural Earth 1:110m geography marine polygons, public domain, retrieved 2026-10-16 from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_geography_marine_polys.geojson . `Sources/EarthCore/Resources/marine-regions.geojson` preserves geometry, retains identifiers and English/Chinese labels, and normalizes several Simplified Chinese ocean names. Caspian Sea is intentionally excluded from ocean reading.

`marine.json` contains original short English and Chinese educational summaries derived from NOAA Fisheries and British Antarctic Survey. Each profile retains its original source URL, including NOAA's western Atlantic tuna guide for Mediterranean spawning. No source images, logos or full articles are redistributed. Latitude gates are editorial reading scopes, not scientific species-distribution polygons or live observations. A country polygon check rejects mapped land; unresolved or unsupported marine areas retain a fallback.

## AIS vessel positions

Optional live data source: AISStream, https://www.aisstream.io/documentation/ (documentation checked 2026-10-16). Uses the user's own account/key; no API credential or live vessel dataset is bundled. The application labels source/report or reception time and displays a sparse, expiring subset, without route extrapolation. Source availability and coverage are not guaranteed. SceneKit boat geometry is generated by this project using its existing felt texture. Diagnostic images use explicitly labeled test fixtures, not observations.
