Little Earth — Free and open source / 羊毛小地球 · 免费开源

Requirements: Apple Silicon Mac (M-series), macOS 14 or later.
系统要求：Apple Silicon（M 系列）Mac，macOS 14 或更新版本。
Intel Macs are not included in this beta / 本轮尚不支持 Intel Mac。

Install: Drag Little Earth into Applications, then open it from Applications.
安装：将 Little Earth 拖入 Applications（应用程序），再从应用程序中打开。

Drag the globe to move it. Hold Option while dragging to spin it.
Click to pause and read. Press Escape or click outside the card to close it.
Right-click for size, language, weather, ship tracking and Quit.
拖动移动；Option + 拖动旋转；点击暂停并阅读；Esc 或点击卡片外收起。
右键菜单可调整大小、语言、天气、船只追踪和退出。

English is the default. Simplified Chinese is available in the right-click menu.
默认英语；右键菜单中可切换简体中文。

Weather displays regional model estimates from Open-Meteo, not satellite imagery.
Ship tracking is optional and requires your own AISStream account and API key.
Keep the key private; enter it only in the app's Ship tracking settings.
No key is needed to enjoy the globe, weather, culture and marine stories.
天气是 Open-Meteo 区域模型数据，不是卫星实况。
船只追踪为可选项，需要自己的 AISStream 账号和 Key；仅在应用设置中填写。
不配置 Key 也可以使用地球、天气、文化介绍和海洋科普。

Weather, vessel coverage and availability depend on the data providers.
The felt map and ship symbols are artistic; do not use them for navigation.
This beta has no automatic updater. Quit the app before replacing it with a later version.
天气和船位覆盖受数据来源影响；毛毡地图与船模为美术表达，不用于导航。
本测试版没有自动更新；安装新版前请先退出旧版。

Feedback: open an issue at https://github.com/Venomn/little-earth/issues with macOS version, Mac model,
steps to reproduce and an optional screenshot. Never include API keys or passwords.
反馈：在 GitHub 提交 Issue，附 macOS 版本、机型、复现步骤及可选截图。
不要发送 API Key 或密码。

Remove a saved AIS key from Ship tracking settings before uninstalling.
Quit the app, then move Little Earth from Applications to Trash.
Local weather cache and preferences may remain; see PRIVACY.txt.
卸载前可在船只设置中移除保存的 Key，然后退出并将应用移入废纸篓。
本地天气缓存和偏好可能保留，详见 PRIVACY.txt。
